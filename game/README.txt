Sanguosha 三国杀 — Learning Table
=================================
How to start: double-click index.html (opens in your web browser; works offline).
The card images are loaded from the "cropped" folder next to this "game" folder — keep both folders together.

Tips
- Hover over any card, general portrait, equipment, or skill button to read its English + Chinese text.
- Your turn: click a card -> click a highlighted target -> Use. Skill buttons sit above your hand.
- "Hint" suggests a move. Green lines in the log explain rules as they happen.
- "Autoplay my seat" lets the computer play for you so you can watch and learn.
- Rules / Cards / Generals buttons open reference panels.
